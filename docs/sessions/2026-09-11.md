## What got built

### 6e. Bottom-right note (new)

A collapsible free-text note in the bottom-right corner, mirroring the stats
widget's chip-and-panel shape so the two bottom corners read as one family.

- Collapsed chip shows `Note` plus the first non-blank line, truncated to 22
  chars with an ellipsis. `min-width: 0` on that span is what makes the
  truncation work — a flex item won't shrink below its content's intrinsic
  width without it, so a long line would widen the whole widget instead.
- Panel holds a `<textarea>` (vertically resizable) and a small save-status
  line that reserves its own height so the panel doesn't jump when the status
  appears and clears.
- Clicking the chip expands and focuses the textarea.

### The heart

`<p class="eyebrow"><span id="stageLabel">Focus</span><span class="heart">♥</span></p>`.
The word is its own span because the render loop rewrites it four times a
second; the heart sits outside it so all three states ("Focus" / "Focusing" /
"Done") keep it without three separate strings having to remember to.

CSS: `color: var(--accent)`, `font-size: 0.9em`, `margin-left: 0.4em` (the
eyebrow's own 0.14em letter-spacing is under 2px at this size and reads as a
typo, not a space), `margin-right: -0.14em` to cancel the phantom trailing
letter-space that would otherwise push the centred label left of centre.

## Decisions made

- **Free text, not a to-do list.** A list carries structure to maintain — add,
  tick, delete, reorder — and all of it is UI competing with the timer. A
  textarea holds a task list, a phone number or half a sentence equally well
  and maintains nothing.
- **Stored in Firebase at `notes/<person>`, not localStorage.** It follows him
  to another browser, another laptop, and across the move from `file://` to the
  GitHub Pages origin — all three mint a fresh, empty localStorage. Not shared
  state: her page never reads it, exactly like `sessions/` and `categories/`.
  This is the **fourth per-person subtree** and needs a fourth rules clause.
- **localStorage still used, as a paint-first mirror.** Without it the box sits
  visibly empty for the few hundred ms the Firebase round trip takes, which
  reads as "my notes are gone" on every load. Cache key `noteCache:<ME>`.
- **Writes debounced 800ms**, not per keystroke — a note is typed in bursts and
  per-character writes would be hundreds of round trips for one line.
- **A remote value is applied only when it cannot destroy work in progress:**
  not while the textarea is focused, and not while a debounced save is pending.
  Queued in `noteRemote` otherwise and applied on blur — without that hook, an
  edit from his other laptop would sit unapplied until a reload, since the
  value subscription has already fired and won't fire again.
- **A keystroke clears any queued remote value.** His typing is newer intent.
  Without this, finishing a sentence and clicking away could restore the
  version the other laptop sent mid-typing.
- **UID debug strip tightened** (`bottom: 0.5rem`, `right: 1.25rem`,
  `line-height: 1`) so it clears the note widget pinned at `bottom: 2rem`.
  13px of gap at 1280×800.

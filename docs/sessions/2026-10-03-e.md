## What got built

`js/shared-today.js`: each laptop publishes today's split to
`room/<me>/today` and holds the other person's. The breakdown panel has a
`You / Kristina` pill; theirs shows today only. Pure shape and validation
in `js/lib/categories.js` (`shareableToday`, `readSharedToday`), plus
`endOfDay` in `js/lib/time.js`. `stats.js` now draws both through one path.

## Decisions made

- **Published summary over cross-reads** (Santi's pick): history stays
  private, no rules change, tiny payload, owner's own calendar day.
- **Today only** (Santi's call); the period pill locks to Today on theirs.
- **Breakdown widget gets the flip**, not the totals chip.
- **`until` = owner's next local midnight**, so a stale summary expires on
  the reader's side without the owner republishing.
- **Labels and colours resolved by the owner**, so the reader never needs
  their vocabulary.
- **Debounced 1s, skipped when unchanged**, so loading the log writes once.
- **Opens on your own split** each load rather than remembering the flip.

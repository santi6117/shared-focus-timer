## Decisions made

- **Sky blended from keyframes**, not four scenes swapped on the hour, so
  it changes continuously while you work.
- **Updated once a minute** via custom properties; sun and moon glide on a
  one-minute transition between updates.
- **Fixed sunrise/sunset schedule**, not from location: the page doesn't
  know where anyone is, and a wallpaper isn't worth a location prompt.
- **Hills are inline SVG** with a baked shadow filter (static, so it costs
  nothing per frame); only clouds and a star twinkle animate.
- `?sky=<hour>` pins the sky for previewing.

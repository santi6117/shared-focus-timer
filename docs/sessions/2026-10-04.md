# Session — 2026-10-04

**Stage:** Stopwatch mode (§6k). Done. Security walkthrough given in chat.

## What got built

A countdown/stopwatch switch beside Start. The stopwatch counts up and
stops itself at two hours, logging two hours and firing the end alert.
`js/lib/timer-math.js` gains `STOPWATCH_CAP_MS` and `shownMs`;
`js/lib/time.js` gains `formatElapsed`; `timer.js` gains `setMode`;
`presence.js`, `remote-view.js` and the notification text know the mode.

## Decisions made

- **Stopwatch = countdown of length 2h showing time used**, over a
  separate counting-up path: every logging rule is reused unchanged.
- **Mode toggle**, not a second start button (Santi's pick).
- **At the cap: same alert as a countdown end** (Santi's pick); the clock
  holds at 2:00:00 rather than counting `+` past it.
- **The peer and the phone count up**, marked ↑ (Santi's pick); costs one
  `mode` field in `room/<me>`, no rules change.
- **Switching mode while paused logs the pending time**, like a duration
  change; the countdown's minutes are kept and come back.
- **Countdown display unchanged** (still `90:00` past the hour).

## Decisions made

- **Top-down, cut paper, calm (4 koi), follows the clock** — Santi's picks.
- **Fish swim as nested transforms** (drift loop → orbit → sway → tail),
  so they always face the way they're going. CSS motion paths rejected:
  not composited in Chrome.
- **Fish homes in the left and right thirds**; a first pass with central
  homes left them under the timer card most of the time.
- **Shadows blurred inside their own SVG**, one fixed offset for the
  school, no sway or tail beat.
- **One multiplied light colour for the whole scene**, not a palette per
  layer. Dusk is lavender: rose on teal cancelled to grey.
- **No transition on the light colour**: a full-screen colour transition
  would repaint every frame; the minute steps are invisible anyway.
- **Sun/moon reflection on Paper hills' arc**, so the two agree on time.

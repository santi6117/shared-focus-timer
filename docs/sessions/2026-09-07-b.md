## Decisions made
- **Visual direction:** warm & cozy — soft paper tones, rounded cards, gentle shadows. Chosen over minimal/dark/terminal.
- **Layout:** timer centered, presence top-right, stats bottom-left. Rejected the single stacked card because it leaves no open middle for the background to show through.
- **Stats display:** collapsed "Today" chip that expands on click into three rows plus a session count. Panel sits above the chip in the DOM so the widget grows upward and the chip stays anchored.
- **Zero cue (closes roadmap §9):** timer counts *upward* past zero in the accent color; run stops, presence flips to "not working", session auto-logs at full duration.
- **Duration change now logs first.** Previously, editing the minutes box after a pause silently discarded elapsed time — the exact thing the tracker exists to prevent.
- **5-second noise floor** on logging, so misclicks don't fill the history.
- **Week starts Monday, local timezone**, computed from the local calendar rather than by subtracting 86,400,000ms (DST would misfile a week's sessions).
- **Background stays a placeholder** — flat warm tone, one custom property, no picker (roadmap §6c/§7).

**Stage:** Second round of features opened (status, phone, wallpapers).

## What got built

A status ("eating", "out with friends") stored at `room/<me>/status` and
shown in the other person's pill with its age, set from a box plus presets
in the timer card. Phone remote mode: `js/device.js` detects a phone, which
then shows the laptop's timer read-only (`js/remote-view.js`), never
publishes a timer or registers `onDisconnect`, and keeps messages sealed
from the published state (`js/own-room.js`).

## Decisions made

- **Timers only on laptops; the phone is a remote** (Santi's call, option B):
  it keeps the phone out of focus sessions.
- **Phone detected by hardware (touch, no hover)**, not width, with a
  remembered `?device=` override.
- **Phone never arms `onDisconnect`**: a locking iPhone would flip you to
  "not working" mid-session.
- **Timer publishes with `update()`** so a laptop pause can't wipe a status
  set from the phone.
- **Status lives in `room/`**, not a new subtree: no rules change needed now.
- **Status cleared by Start, kept on disconnect, shown with age, no expiry.**
- **Presets: eating, sleeping, out with friends, errands, in lectures.**
  Shown on focus on a laptop, always on a phone.
- **Pinned rules now take a phone UID per person** (four placeholders).
- Santi said the roadmap's limits are guidance: do what's best and keep it
  accessible to him (recorded in §2).
- Wallpapers: own hours; Rainy window 100h, Paper hills live sky 200h, Koi
  pond 300h; couples wallpaper at 1,000 combined hours later.

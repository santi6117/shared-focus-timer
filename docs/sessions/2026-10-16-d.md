# Session — 2026-10-16 (d)

**Stage:** Reversible time management implemented for local review.

## What got built

Replaced the Time management placeholder with a collapsed session-history
panel. It adds missed work, edits duration/end time/timezone/category, reviews
removal before saving, restores removed records and undoes changes across
reloads. History is paged five records at a time. Drafts survive navigation;
validation rejects future times, invalid zones, daylight-saving gaps/repeated
hours and durations outside one second to 24 hours.

`js/time-management.js` owns the UI. `js/lib/session-edits.js` validates wall
times and materializes edit snapshots. `js/sessions.js` provides coherent
history and version-checked Firebase transactions. Original record fields
remain intact; `timeEdits` append snapshots, `activeEdit` selects the effective
state, and monotonic `editVersion` detects concurrent edits. Manual work has a
hidden base so Undo can also undo additions. Failed saves keep the draft for
retry. Firebase callbacks abort conflicts rather than throwing from retries.

Totals, breakdowns and wallpaper progress consume nonremoved effective
sessions. Calendar reconciliation sees all history, including removed records,
and keeps hashed consent markers while contributing zero seconds/count for
removed work. Correcting unshared legacy history does not opt it into sharing.
Removing the final session now also clears the existing shared-today summary.

## Decisions made

Santi explicitly chose adding, editing and removing logged time, with undo and
original records preserved. Corrections live inside existing session records,
so no new top-level Firebase grants are needed. Editing requires connectivity;
a stale form reports a conflict instead of overwriting another device's work.
Manual additions intentionally share the entered day's total, as stated in
the form; older private sessions still require explicit calendar backfill.
The feature remains local for preview; do not push, merge or deploy this new
work until Santi has reviewed it. Main and production Firebase are untouched.

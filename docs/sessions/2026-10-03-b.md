# Session — 2026-10-03 (b)

**Stage:** Unlockable wallpapers — system and the first wallpaper (Rainy
window) built. Waiting on Santi's feedback before Paper hills.

## What got built

`js/lib/wallpapers.js` (catalogue, unlocks from hours, which one to show)
and `js/wallpapers.js` (applies the choice, `?bg=` preview, a picker inside
the stats panel, a new-unlock dot on the stats chip). Each wallpaper is a
`.wp-<id>` wrapper in `#backgroundLayer`, chosen by `data-bg`. The first
new one, `css/wallpapers/rain.css`: an evening city out of focus behind wet
glass, pure CSS.

## Decisions made

- **Unlocks derived from the session log**, nothing stored.
- **Choice per device in localStorage**, trusted on load so the right
  wallpaper paints on the first frame.
- **Picker in the stats panel**; locked rows show "62 / 100 h"; unbuilt
  ones are listed so there's something to aim for.
- **Thumbnails are static palettes**, not live renders.
- **Hidden wallpapers are `display: none`**, so only one animates.
- **No picker on the phone** for now.
- Found, not fixed: the swirl's centre wash sits under its blobs, so
  `--bg-veil` does nothing. Fixing it would change the look Santi tuned.
